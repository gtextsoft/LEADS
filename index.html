Why this evening

This isn't a seminar. It's a room.

— and the room is the point.

You've sat through enough panels and replayed enough webinars. This is the opposite of that. A deliberate gathering of operators — people who sign payroll, chase invoices, and lie awake over growth — in conversation with someone who has built and backed businesses through every stage of it.

You'll leave with names worth keeping, a sharper read on where money is moving, and an honest answer or two to the questions you can't ask a slide deck.

Your host for the evening

"Wealth follows the people who do the unglamorous work consistently. My job is to put the right rooms in front of them."

Dr. Stephen Akintayo is an entrepreneur, investment coach, and the founder of SACI Holdings — with interests spanning real estate, investment advisory, and enterprise across Africa and the diaspora. Over the years he has mentored a generation of business owners on building assets, raising capital, and thinking past the next quarter.

In Leeds, he's trading the stage for a seat at the table — close enough to ask the real questions and get a straight answer.
